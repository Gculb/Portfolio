A retrieval-grounded EMS training loop that combines protocol retrieval with a rule-based evaluation gate to reduce hallucination risk and preserve safety-critical intent.

Section-level chunking retained protocol flow while keeping retrieval concise. Multilingual-e5-large embeddings improved clinical terminology coverage and ranking quality.

Architecture compresses to a real-time pipeline where user actions flow from browser → Socket.IO → SimulationOrchestrator → RAG retrieval → grounded response synthesis.

Core flow

  1. Trainee action captured via UI or speech intent.
  2. Rule-based evaluation validates decisions before state changes.
  3. State update adjusts vitals, symptoms, and scenario progress.
  4. RAG response retrieves protocol context and generates grounded patient dialogue.
  5. Scenario debrief delivers outcome and audit trail after the simulation concludes.

SignalProcessor

End-to-end DSP pipeline from Python prototyping to embedded Arduino deployment. Designed to move signal-processing research from notebook to production with measurable SNR improvements.

Designed and implemented a full DSP pipeline from Python experimentation to Arduino deployment.

  • Python prototype: FFT inspection, filtering, denoising, waveform/spectrum views, and live Web Audio API capture for experimentation.
  • Measured on recorded speech samples: raw audio 1.8 dB SNR → 4.93 dB SNR post-pipeline.

Public health data · Virginia & Roanoke City (2016–2023)

Regional Health Analysis

Evidence-based epidemiological analysis surfacing structural health disparities using age-adjusted rates and longitudinal trend data. Built to make invisible inequity visible through rigorous statistical methodology.

Epidemiological analysis of chronic disease hospitalization data across Virginia and the Roanoke City Health District, using age-adjusted rates, distribution comparisons, and longitudinal trend analysis to surface structural health disparities across six major chronic conditions.

  • Analyzed CVD, diabetes, COPD, asthma, hypertension, and CKD hospitalization rates from the Virginia Department of Health dataset (2016–2023).
  • Applied age-adjusted rate normalization to enable valid cross-region comparisons.
  • Built an interactive dashboard to visualize trends and regional disparities over time.

#1 CVD & hypertension — leading causes of hospitalization in both regions

6 Chronic conditions analyzed: CVD, diabetes, COPD, asthma, hypertension, CKD

Persistent regional disparity across all 8 years

Roanoke City consistently showed higher age-adjusted hospitalization rates than Virginia statewide for most chronic conditions from 2016 through 2023. Despite a decline after 2019, the city never closed the gap — indicating structural rather than incidental inequity.

CVD and hypertension dominate both regions

Cardiovascular disease and hypertension represent the highest hospitalization burden in both Virginia and Roanoke City. Diabetes and high blood cholesterol consistently rank among the top contributors — forming a cluster of interrelated metabolic conditions.

Greater variability in Roanoke signals instability

Box-and-whisker distributions show wider spread in Roanoke City compared to the more stable statewide average. Higher variability suggests a less predictable hospitalization burden — consistent with populations at elevated risk for preventable admissions.

Age-adjusted rates enable fair regional comparison

Raw hospitalization counts alone are misleading because hospitalization risk scales with age. This analysis used age-adjusted rates per 100,000 to normalize for demographic differences, making Roanoke–Virginia comparisons methodologically valid rather than superficial.
