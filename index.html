01 / About

Who I Am

I'm a software engineer with a biochemistry foundation — a combination that shapes how I approach every system I build. My undergraduate work in biochemistry gave me a deep appreciation for molecular complexity; my graduate work in CS gave me the tools to model it computationally.

My primary project is Alexandria Virtual Patient, an AI-powered EMS simulation platform with a TypeScript backend, RAG pipeline over ChromaDB, multilingual embeddings, real-time Socket.IO architecture, and an LLM-driven progress evaluator agent.

On the data science side I apply supervised ML to bioinformatics problems — protein sequence classification, regional health analysis, and Virginia public health datasets — using Python's scientific stack.

BiochemReact is my flagship open-source project: an interactive biochemistry learning platform with 3D molecular visualization, an enzyme kinetics simulator, AlphaFold integration, and 35 topic categories.
