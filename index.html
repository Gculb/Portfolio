Statistical significance is not biological importance.

Adjusted p-values summarize evidence under a model and account for multiple testing; they do not measure effect size or tell us whether a change matters biologically. That judgment also needs the size and uncertainty of the effect, biological context, replication, and follow-up validation. With four donor pairs, zero genes passing the threshold does not show that treatment has no biological effect.

Exploratory RNA-seq reanalysis and regional benchmark results; these figures do not establish broad biological or clinical validity.

  5. Scenario debrief delivers outcome and audit trail after the simulation concludes.

Curiosity-driven scientific exploration · Signal processing

SignalProcessor

Project type: Independent exploration Stack: Python, NumPy, SciPy, FastAPI, scikit-learn, Web Audio API Question: How much can simple DSP improve noisy speech, and what trade-offs appear in real-time processing?

A hands-on investigation of noisy audio: inspect it, try filtering and denoising methods, compare the results, and see how much of the pipeline can fit on constrained hardware.

Approach

Used FFT and waveform views to inspect recordings, then explored filtering, denoising, smoothing, and gating in Python.

Observation

On the recorded speech sample used in the prototype, measured SNR moved from 1.8 dB before processing to 4.93 dB after processing. One real-time implementation measured 18 ms latency.

  • Built a browser-based workbench for live microphone capture, waveform/spectrum inspection, and listening to processed audio.
  • Added a small-data classifier experiment with a review step for labeled and unlabeled WAV examples.
  • Kept the FastAPI processing and retraining endpoints as a way to compare the Python prototype with the browser-based workbench.
  • Scope: these measurements are exploratory observations from prototype recordings, not a general benchmark of speech enhancement quality.

Persistent regional disparity across all 8 years

Roanoke City consistently showed higher age-adjusted hospitalization rates than Virginia statewide for most chronic conditions from 2016 through 2023. Despite a decline after 2019, the city never closed the gap — indicating structural rather than incidental inequity.

CVD and hypertension dominate both regions

Cardiovascular disease and hypertension represent the highest hospitalization burden in both Virginia and Roanoke City. Diabetes and high blood cholesterol consistently rank among the top contributors — forming a cluster of interrelated metabolic conditions.

Greater variability in Roanoke signals instability

Box-and-whisker distributions show wider spread in Roanoke City compared to the more stable statewide average. Higher variability suggests a less predictable hospitalization burden — consistent with populations at elevated risk for preventable admissions.

Age-adjusted rates enable fair regional comparison

Raw hospitalization counts alone are misleading because hospitalization risk scales with age. This analysis used age-adjusted rates per 100,000 to normalize for demographic differences, making Roanoke–Virginia comparisons methodologically valid rather than superficial.
